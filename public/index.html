About Last.fm Comparator

⚠️ Important: Community rankings are based on unique listener count of all time, while your rankings are based on your play count. These are different metrics, and this may produce unexpected results. This is a limitation of the last.fm API, which does not support date filtering or sorting by play count for an artist's top tracks.

How it works

We rank the top 100 tracks by an artist (by community unique listeners) and compare against your personal ranking (by your play counts). The differential is the difference between these ranks.

  • Positive Differential: Your "unusual favourites" - songs you play more than most.
  • Negative Differential: Songs more popular with others than with you.
  • Near Zero: Your taste aligns with the community.

Limitations

  • Fetching Time: Loading your scrobble history may take a few minutes due to API rate limits.
  • Recency Bias: Newer releases may appear in "unusual favourites" simply because many older accounts haven't scrobbled them.
